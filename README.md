# Company Insights 360° 📊

A complete Business Analytics project built using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, products, campaigns, returns, payments, and business trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

**Company Insights 360°** provides a 360-degree view of business performance by combining data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL-based business analysis, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The project analyzes **2,000 customer sessions/transactions** across multiple dimensions including:

- Customers
- Sales & Revenue
- Products & Categories
- Geography
- Marketing Campaigns
- Customer Conversion
- Returns
- Payment Methods
- Time-based trends

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify profitable products and categories
- Understand customer demographics and behavior
- Analyze customer conversion and purchase journeys
- Evaluate marketing campaign performance
- Analyze product returns and return reasons
- Identify popular payment methods
- Understand geographical sales patterns
- Identify time-based sales and order trends
- Generate actionable business recommendations
